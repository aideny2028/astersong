# advisor-outreach.md

The advisor seat on both the launch and goal sites is **open** on purpose. This
is the honest way to fill it. Rule: no real person's name goes on any shipped
page until they have said yes, in writing, to exactly what you are asking.

## What you are asking for
One reviewer who will check your cross-identifications and method, and lend real
rigor. You are asking for **review and guidance**, not a co-authorship, not an
endorsement they have not given, not their institution's name as a sponsor.

## Who fits
- Historians of astronomy / archaeoastronomy (people who work on exactly the
  guest-star and eclipse records you sonify).
- Time-domain / supernova-remnant astronomers (people who can sanity-check a
  cross-ID like SN 1054 to the Crab, SN 185 to RCW 86).
- Authors of the papers your cross-identifications already cite. If you rely on
  someone's work, they are a natural, well-matched first email.

Build the list with the CRCS professor-cold-email finder. Aim for 15 to 20
well-matched names, ranked by fit and reply likelihood.

## The email (honest template)
- Say who you are in one line: a high-school student who built an open-source
  tool.
- Say what it is in two lines: Xuanji, a deterministic library that sonifies
  reconstructed light curves of historical astronomical records; sources cited,
  uncertainty kept.
- Link the repo and one audio example. Let the work speak.
- Make the ask small and specific: "Would you be willing to review the ten
  cross-identifications in the launch release, and tell me where I have
  overstated a link?" Small asks get answered.
- Do not offer authorship. Do not imply they already support the project.

## When someone says yes
- Confirm in writing what they agreed to (review only, or a named advisor role).
- Only then add their name, and only in the form they approved. Update
  `AUTHORS.md` and the site's advisor line together.
- If they decline or go quiet, the seat stays open. An open seat is honest; a
  fabricated name is not.

## Hard line
Prof. the research mentor (or any real person discussed in planning) is **not** named on
the site and is not an advisor unless and until they personally agree in writing.
Until that happens, the site says "Open." This is non-negotiable (CRCS rule
12au, the honesty firewall in `../provenance.md`).
