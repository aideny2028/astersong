# IMAGE-CREDITS.md, Xuanji sonification docs site

Every real photograph woven through this site is free-license (public domain,
CC BY, or CC BY-SA). Each entry below gives the file, what it shows, the source,
and the license line the license requires. The footer of every page points here.

The waveform and spectrogram plates in `assets/img/fig/` are **not** in this
list: they are generated by the reference sonification engine from reconstructed
light curves, not third-party images. See `provenance.md`.

> Release note: this is an illustrative sample build. Before any public
> deployment, the maintainer must re-confirm each line below against the exact
> file as downloaded (the source page, the specific image ID, and the license
> version in force on the download date), and update any that have changed.
> Where a CC license applies, keep the attribution visible at point of use.

## Photographs in `assets/img/photo/`

### crab.jpg
- Shows: the Crab Nebula, the remnant of **SN 1054** in Taurus.
- Source: NASA, ESA, and STScI (Hubble Space Telescope composite).
- License: **Public domain** (NASA/ESA Hubble imagery). Credit line:
  "Image: NASA / ESA / STScI."

### sn1006.jpg
- Shows: the remnant of **SN 1006** in Lupus, the brightest recorded stellar
  event.
- Source: NASA / CXC (Chandra X-ray Observatory) multi-wavelength composite.
- License: **Public domain** (NASA/CXC imagery). Credit line:
  "Image: NASA / CXC / SAO."

### kepler_sn.jpg
- Shows: the remnant of **SN 1604**, Kepler's supernova.
- Source: NASA, ESA, and the Johns Hopkins University (Chandra / Hubble /
  Spitzer composite).
- License: **Public domain** (NASA imagery). Credit line:
  "Image: NASA / ESA / JHU."

### dunhuang.jpg
- Shows: a panel of the **Dunhuang star chart** (~700 CE), one of the oldest
  known manuscript sky atlases.
- Source: The British Library (Dunhuang manuscript, Or.8210/S.3326), photographic
  reproduction.
- License: **Public domain** (original work by age; faithful photographic
  reproduction of a 2-D public-domain work). Credit line:
  "Original: British Library, Dunhuang collection. Public domain."

### suzhou.jpg
- Shows: a rubbing of the **Suzhou (Soochow) planisphere**, engraved **1247 CE**
  (drafted c. 1193).
- Source: Wikimedia Commons, ink rubbing of the stone stele.
- License: **Public domain** (original work by age). Credit line:
  "Suzhou planisphere rubbing, 1247 CE. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons."

### halley.jpg
- Shows: **Halley's Comet** at its 1986 apparition.
- Source: NASA / NSSDC (W. Liller, International Halley Watch Large Scale
  Phenomena Network).
- License: **Public domain** (NASA imagery). Credit line:
  "Image: NASA / W. Liller / NSSDC."

### milkyway.jpg
- Shows: the arch of the **Milky Way** over a dark-sky site.
- Source: European Southern Observatory (ESO).
- License: **CC BY 4.0**. Credit line:
  "Image: ESO / [photographer], CC BY 4.0." Keep the attribution and a link to
  the ESO source page at point of use.

### radiotelescope.jpg
- Shows: a large steerable **radio telescope** (Parkes / Murriyang class dish).
- Source: CSIRO, via Wikimedia Commons.
- License: **CC BY 3.0**. Credit line:
  "Image: CSIRO, CC BY 3.0." Keep the attribution and a link to the source page
  at point of use.
